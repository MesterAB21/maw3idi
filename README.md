 
# MAW3IDI — Intelligent Appointment Management

**AI-Powered Appointment Management for Healthcare**

this is the front end repo, the backend and agent repo are private

MAW3IDI is an intelligent appointment management project designed for medical and paramedical practices in Algeria. It focuses on simplifying appointment coordination and facilitating patient communication through intelligent automation.

## Features

- **24/7 Virtual Secretary** — Assist with appointment-related requests and patient inquiries at any time.
- **Appointment Management** — Facilitate appointment booking, scheduling, and coordination.
- **Multichannel Communication** — Support interactions through phone calls, WhatsApp, and Instagram.
- **Intelligent Conversations** — Provide contextual responses to frequently asked questions about services, availability, and practice information.
- **French & Algerian Darija** — Enable more natural and accessible communication with patients.
- **Administrative Assistance** — Automate routine tasks while complementing the work of existing healthcare staff.
- **Human Oversight** — Keep healthcare professionals in control of their practice and administrative decisions.

## Target Applications

- Medical practices and specialist doctors
- Dental clinics
- Psychologists and therapists
- Paramedical practices
- Private healthcare establishments

## Vision

MAW3IDI explores the application of artificial intelligence and automation in healthcare administration, with a focus on accessible, multilingual, and intelligent patient interactions adapted to the Algerian healthcare environment.
